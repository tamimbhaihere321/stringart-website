About the artist

Elena Vance

Thread artist & storyteller based in the Pacific Northwest.

What started as a lockdown hobby quickly became a passion. I love the meditative process of wrapping thread around nails – each piece grows slowly, one line at a time. My style blends organic curves with structured geometry, using premium cotton thread and hand‑finished wooden boards.

I believe that string art is more than decor: it’s a way to hold memories, names, and shapes in a tangible, textured form. Every custom order begins with a conversation about what matters to you.
